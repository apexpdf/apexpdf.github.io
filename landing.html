Features

Built for real paperwork

Twelve tools that cover the whole lifecycle of a document — from first draft to signed archive.

Click any line. Retype it. Done.

The editor detects each line's font family, size, weight and color, then bakes your change straight into the PDF. Fix a typo in a contract, swap a date, or erase a stamp — with instant undo.

  • Same-font matching (sans / serif / mono + bold / italic)
  • Add text, images, or erase areas like QR codes
  • Thumbnail sidebar, zoom, and page navigation
Try the editor

Merge, split, reorder in seconds

Combine ten invoices into one file. Pull three pages out of a 200-page report. Rotate sideways scans, compress a 40 MB deck to mailable size, stamp page numbers.

  • Merge, split to ZIP, delete / extract / reorder pages
  • Compress, rotate, watermark and page numbers
  • Handles files up to ~100 MB in the browser
Combine files

Scans become searchable text

OCR reads photographed pages and scanned PDFs in eight languages, then hands you clean text to copy or download. Convert the result to Word, HTML, CSV, JPG or PNG.

  • Tesseract OCR in English, Spanish, French, German + more
  • Export to DOCX, TXT, HTML, CSV or page images
  • JPG/PNG to PDF for phone-camera captures
Extract text

Private by architecture, not by promise

There is no upload server to breach: pdf-lib, pdf.js and Tesseract all run locally on your machine. Legal, medical and financial documents stay exactly where they belong.

  • No accounts, no tracking cookies on documents
  • No watermarks stamped on your output
  • Open source-friendly, auditable client-side code
